Step by step

What actually happens.

Each step has a purpose and an end. We move on when it is met — not on a schedule invented in advance.

  1. 01 — Listen

    Start with the problem.

    We ask what you are trying to solve, who it is for and what “working” would look like. No solutions yet.

  2. 02 — Sketch

    Ideas before execution.

    Rough directions, quickly. Cheap to change now, expensive to change later.

  3. 03 — Shape

    Find the clearest direction.

    We choose one direction, say why, and agree the scope before anything is built.

  4. 04 — Build

    Design. Code. Create.

    The work gets made in visible stages, so there are no surprises at the end.

  5. 05 — Refine

    Test. Improve. Polish.

    We check it on real devices, fix what is awkward and sharpen the details.

  6. 06 — Deliver

    Something ready to use.

    Handover with what you need to run it — files, access and a walkthrough.

Start with an idea.

  • Make it clearer.
  • Shape the direction.
  • Build what matters.
  • Refine the details.
  • Make it useful.
  • Make it memorable.
