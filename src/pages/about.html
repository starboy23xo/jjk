Principles

What we believe.

Four sentences we keep coming back to when a decision is difficult.

  • Ideas should be understood.

    Before anything is designed, the idea has to be clear to both of us.

  • Technology should feel useful.

    If it doesn’t make something easier, it doesn’t belong in the build.

  • Design should have a reason.

    Every element earns its place, or it comes off the page.

  • Details matter.

    The last ten per cent is what people actually feel.
